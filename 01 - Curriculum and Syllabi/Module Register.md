## About the LTP column (unverified)

> [!caution] Do not rely on an interpretation of LTP until it is confirmed
> In many technical-education systems, "LTP" means **L**ecture, **T**utorial and **P**ractical contact hours per week. That reading is plausible here: Information System Project Management has P = 0, and Cyber Security has the largest practical value (4). **However, this has not been verified** for your institution. The columns might instead count hours per semester, credit components, or something else. Ask your department, or check the official programme document, then record the answer in [[Syllabus Verification Log]].

### How the vault uses LTP (only as a planning hint)

The [[Weekly Study Planner]] gives practical-heavy modules more hands-on time (Cyber Security labs, Python coding). It does this because practice dominates competence in those subjects, **not** because of any assumed meaning of LTP. If the confirmed meaning differs, you only need to adjust the planner.
