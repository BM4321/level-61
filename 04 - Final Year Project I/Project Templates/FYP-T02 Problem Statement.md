## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every factual claim has a source (my data with n, or a citation I have read)
- [ ] It describes a problem, not the absence of my solution ("there is no app" is not a problem)
- [ ] The consequences are concrete
- [ ] The purpose sentence matches my main research question
