## 1. Does my project need ethics approval?
- [ ] It involves human participants (interviews, surveys, usability tests, observation)
- [ ] It uses personal data (including from a gatekeeper's records)
- [ ] It involves vulnerable people, minors, or people in a dependent relationship with me

If any box is ticked, assume approval is needed, and confirm the process with your supervisor and the ethics committee.
