## 1. Purpose and business justification
Replace queue-based paper registration with online registration. The current process costs registry staff overtime, causes timetable clashes, and creates fee disputes. The business case (Option A, re-appraised in PM-09 at a 60 M cost baseline with 22 M a year of benefits): NPV TZS 6.8 M at 12%, IRR 17.3%, payback 2.7 years.

## 2. Measurable objectives and success criteria
| Level | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Project | Live by week 19 (before semester start, week 22); within the 60 M baseline; all Must requirements accepted |
| Product | 95% of students register online in semester 1; p95 page time ≤ 2 s at 2,000 concurrent users |
| Business | Registry queue time −80%; zero timetable clashes for registered students; staff overtime −60% |

## 3. High-level scope
**In:** online registration, clash detection, course catalogue, migration of current enrolments, registry admin screens, training, go-live support.
**Out:** mobile app, fee payment integration, SMS reminders, timetable creation tool.

## 5. Summary budget
Work packages 54 M + contingency 6 M = **cost baseline 60 M**; management reserve 4 M (held by the sponsor).

## 6. Key risks, assumptions and constraints
- **Risks:** server delivery delay; poor data quality; key developer leaving; rule changes; peak-load outage (see [[PM-A06 Risk Register]]).
- **Assumptions:** registry staff available 2 days a week; registration rules frozen at design approval.
- **Constraints:** fixed go-live before semester; public procurement rules for the servers.

## 7. Key stakeholders
Sponsor; registrar; registry staff; heads of department; students; ICT director; server vendor (see [[PM-A07 Stakeholder Register]]).

## 8. Project manager's authority
- Reallocate up to 5% of the budget between work packages.
- Approve changes under 1 week or 2% of the budget.
- Escalate anything larger to the steering committee.
