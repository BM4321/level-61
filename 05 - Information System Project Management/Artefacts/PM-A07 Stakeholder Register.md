# PM-A07 Stakeholder Register

> [!info] How to use
> Filled in for [[PM-CASE-01 University Course Registration System]]. Lesson: [[PM-12 Stakeholder and Communication Management]]. Engagement: U unaware, R resistant, N neutral, S supportive, L leading; C = current, D = desired.

| Stakeholder | Role / interest | Power | Interest | Quadrant | C | D | Strategy | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deputy VC Academic | Sponsor; strategic success | High | High | Manage closely | S | L | Monthly steering; early warnings; decisions | PM |
| Registrar | Process owner; UAT accountable | High | High | Manage closely | N | S | Co-design workshops; owns acceptance | PM |
| Registry clerks | Daily users; job concerns | Low | High | Keep informed | R | S | Champions; demos; training; job-security message | BA |
| Heads of department | Course capacity control | High | Low | Keep satisfied | U | N | Senate brief; configurable capacity | Sponsor |
| Students (union) | Easy, fair registration | Low individually, high collectively | High | Keep informed (watch) | U | S | Notices, SMS, FAQ, union briefing | Registrar's office |
| ICT director | Resources, security, operations | High | Medium | Keep satisfied | S | S | Architecture reviews; resource agreement | PM |
| Server vendor | Contract delivery | Medium | High | Keep informed | S | S | Weekly calls; contract reviews | Procurement officer |

Review it monthly, and record any change of engagement level with the date.
