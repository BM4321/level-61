# PM-A08 Communication Plan

> [!info] How to use
> Filled in for [[PM-CASE-01 University Course Registration System]]. Lessons: [[PM-12 Stakeholder and Communication Management]], [[PM-15 Team Leadership and Conflict Management]] (RACI).

## 1. Communication matrix
| Audience | Information | Method | Frequency | Sender | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering committee | Status, EVM, top risks, decisions needed | Meeting + [[PM-A09 Status Report]] | Monthly + on exception | PM | Minuted decisions |
| Sponsor | Escalations, decisions | Call / one-to-one | As needed (within 24 h for red items) | PM | Decision log |
| Project team | Tasks, blockers | Daily stand-up; task board | Daily | Scrum master | Action items |
| Registry staff | Demos, training, changes | Sprint review; email | Every 2 weeks | BA | Q&A; survey |
| Students | What, when, how, help | Portal, SMS, student union, FAQ (EN + SW) | 4 weeks, 1 week, and the day before go-live | Registrar's office | Help desk |
| Heads of department | Capacity settings, timeline | Senate brief; one-page summary | Twice (design, pre-go-live) | Sponsor | Senate minutes |
| Vendor | Delivery, technical issues | Call; email | Weekly | Procurement officer | Contract log |

## 2. RACI (extract)
| Deliverable | PM | BA (registry) | Lead dev | Tester | Registrar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requirements | A | R | C | C | C |
| Design | A | C | R | I | I |
| Migration | A | R | R | C | C |
| UAT sign-off | R | C | I | R | **A** |
| Training | A | R | C | I | I |

## 3. Escalation path
Team member → PM (same day) → steering committee (next meeting, or within 48 h for red items) → sponsor.

## 4. Crisis communication (go-live)
Portal banner within 15 min · SMS to students with the revised slots · the steering committee informed by phone · hourly updates · a summary within 48 h.
