# Weak Area Tracker

A topic goes here when a review scores below 50% ([[Review Schedule]]), a mistake recurs ([[Mistake Log]]), or a mock section scores below 50%. It leaves only when the exit test is passed.

**A good plan is specific:** not "revise CPM", but "redo PM-06 Examples 1–3 closed book; do practice L3–L4; explain float aloud; then do the CPM question of MOCK-PM-01 in 15 minutes".

**A good exit test is objective:** the lesson's self-test ≥ 80% *and* one unseen L3–L4 problem correct, closed book, at least 3 days after the last study session.
